The Guide

What is a larp wallet?

A larp wallet is an app that reproduces the interface of a real cryptocurrency wallet while letting you decide what the balances say. It looks like the wallet everyone recognises. It behaves like it too — prices move, charts scrub, notifications arrive. What it does not do is touch a blockchain.

The name comes from LARP — live action role play. Somewhere along the way crypto Twitter borrowed it as shorthand for playing a part, and "larping as a whale" became the phrase for looking richer on screen than you are in reality. A larp wallet is simply the prop that makes the part convincing.

What people actually use them for

  • Content. Short-form video needs a wallet on screen for maybe four seconds. Filming a real one means exposing real balances and real addresses.
  • Thumbnails and screenshots. A portfolio shot that fits the story you are telling, without editing a screenshot pixel by pixel in Photoshop.
  • Jokes. Showing a friend a balance that does not exist is an old bit, and it lands better when the app looks right.
  • Mockups and demos. Designers and educators who need a realistic wallet UI to point at without funding a live account.

What separates a good one from a bad one

Most free "fake wallet" apps fall apart on the details, and the details are the entire product. The things worth checking:

  • Live prices. If the token prices are frozen, anyone who glances at a real chart will spot it instantly.
  • A working chart. Static images do not survive a screen recording. Charts should scrub under your finger.
  • Correct typography and spacing. The gap between "close enough" and "identical" is mostly font weights and padding.
  • Home screen install. An app that opens in a browser with a URL bar at the top is not going to convince anybody.
  • Custom tokens. Being locked to Bitcoin and Ethereum is limiting — memecoins are usually the point.

Larpz Wallet was built around those five things. It is a progressive web app, so it installs to your home screen and opens full screen with no browser chrome. Prices come from CoinGecko in real time. Any Solana, Ethereum or Robinhood Chain token can be added by contract address, and the app pulls the live price and logo for it automatically.

Four Skins

Every licence includes all four

You are not picking one and paying again for the next. A single key unlocks every skin, and you can switch between them whenever you like.

Phantom style

The Solana wallet everyone knows. Add any SPL or ERC-20 token by contract address and the app fetches its live price and image. Interactive 24-hour chart, swap screen, and a full activity history.

Trust style

Multi-chain layout with Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, Solana, Tron and more sitting side by side. Includes a working QR receive screen and per-network address display. Read more.

Ledger style

The hardware wallet look, in software. Account list, market movers, a Fear & Greed gauge and a per-coin price chart with a full transaction history. Read more.

Exodus style

Portfolio-first, built around a donut breakdown of your holdings with a smooth reveal animation. Clean, colourful, and the most distinctive of the four on camera. Read more.
